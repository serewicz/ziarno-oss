# Programs

We want to be open about how Ziarno Open Source works, including how it pays for itself.

## Free

- **All course and curriculum content in this repository** is free and openly licensed. Anyone can use it, change it, and teach it.
- **A free one-day Saturday session, once a month,** for members of a nation's technology leadership, such as ministry, agency, or national IT office leaders. We ask for a ministry email address or a short nomination letter so seats go to people who can lead change.

## Paid

- **Additional instructor-led training** in other courses, for countries that want to train more people. Prices are reasonable and can scale with a country's income level.

## Introductions

Depending on what a country needs, we may introduce them to other trusted organizations for training, support, or services. When we make an introduction, we may receive a referral fee from that organization. We will always tell you when that applies. We'll also stay involved to make sure you get what you paid for.

## Your choice

You never have to buy from us or from anyone we introduce. The content is free, and you are welcome to find your own trainers and partners. The goal is your country's independence, including independence from us.
