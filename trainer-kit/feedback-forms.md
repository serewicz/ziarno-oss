# Feedback forms

Print these or copy them into a free form tool.

## End-of-day form (participants)
1. Course: 1 / 2 / 3
2. How useful was today for your work? 1 2 3 4 5
3. How ready do you feel to teach this to co-workers? 1 2 3 4 5
4. Which lab was most useful?
5. Which step in any lab confused you? (Lab and step number)
6. What should we cut or shorten?
7. What is missing?
8. Anything else?

## Peer feedback form (Course 3, Lab 3)
- Teacher's name:
- One thing that worked:
- One thing to change:
- Did you learn the goal they stated? Yes / Partly / No

## Cascade follow-up (send 30 days after)
1. Did you run your session? Yes / Not yet
2. If yes: date, number of people, which course
3. If not yet: what got in the way?
4. What did you change in the material?
5. Is anyone from your session ready to become a trainer?
