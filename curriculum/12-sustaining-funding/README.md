# Module 12: Sustaining and funding open source

**Level:** Leader  
**Time:** about 3 hours  
**Needs:** a laptop or a phone with a browser; some exercises need an internet connection once.

## Learning objectives
By the end you will be able to:
- Name ways open source is funded
- Sketch a local open-source services business
- Find a grant to apply for

## Lesson outline
1. Funding sources: grants, sponsorship, government contracts, support services
2. Local services businesses: install, host, train, support
3. Transparent money: Open Collective
4. Grant programs: NLnet, Sovereign Tech Agency
5. Paying maintainers, not just users
6. Keeping a project alive when the founder leaves

## Hands-on exercise: Sketch a services business
1. Open https://opensource.guide/getting-paid/ and read it.
2. Pick one open-source tool used in your country.
3. On one page write: who would pay, what you would sell (install, hosting, training, support), rough monthly price in local money, and who would do the work.
4. Open https://nlnet.nl/propose/ and read what they fund and the questions they ask.
5. Write a three-sentence pitch you could use for a grant or a client.

## Quiz
1. Name three ways open source gets funded.
2. What does a local services business sell?
3. Why fund maintainers?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Grants, sponsorship, paid services, government contracts.
2. Installation, hosting, training, and support.
3. Without them projects stop getting fixes and security updates.

</details>
