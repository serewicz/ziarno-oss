# Module 11: Teaching adults and running train-the-trainer cascades

**Level:** Leader  
**Time:** about 5 hours  
**Needs:** a laptop or a phone with a browser; some exercises need an internet connection once.

## Learning objectives
By the end you will be able to:
- Plan a short lesson with a clear goal and a hands-on part
- Check that people learned, not just listened
- Run a cascade where each trainer trains others

## Lesson outline
1. How adults learn: start with their problem, keep talks short, practice right away
2. Live coding and pair work
3. Formative checks: quick questions every 15 minutes
4. Running a lab with mixed skill levels: helpers and sticky notes
5. Cascade: train, practice, co-teach, teach alone
6. Builds on [course 03](../../courses/03-how-to-train/) and the [trainer kit](../../trainer-kit/)

## Hands-on exercise: Teach a 10-minute lesson
1. Pick one exercise from any module in this curriculum.
2. Write one sentence: "After 10 minutes, learners will be able to ...".
3. Plan: 2 minutes explain, 6 minutes practice, 2 minutes check.
4. Write one check question with a clear answer.
5. Teach it to two or three colleagues. Ask them to put up a hand when stuck.
6. Ask the check question. Count correct answers.
7. Fill in the form in [trainer-kit/feedback-forms.md](../../trainer-kit/feedback-forms.md).
8. Add your next session to your plan in [trainer-kit/cascade-plan.md](../../trainer-kit/cascade-plan.md).

## Quiz
1. How long should you talk before learners practice?
2. What is a formative check?
3. What are the steps of a cascade?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. A few minutes; keep it short.
2. A quick question during the lesson to see if people understood.
3. Train, practice, co-teach, teach alone.

</details>
