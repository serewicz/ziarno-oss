# Ziarno Open Source

*Plant open source, grow it local.*

Free, open training that helps technology leaders in emerging countries train others, and move their countries from big-tech dependence to open source.

## How to start

See [PROGRAMS.md](PROGRAMS.md) for free and paid programs, and [HOW-TO-START.md](HOW-TO-START.md) for the national playbook: start with practitioners, set up a national open source office, then move the lowest-risk functions first.

## Why it matters
When a country's schools, governments, and businesses run on closed, foreign-owned tools, they pay ongoing license fees, lose control of their data, and depend on decisions made elsewhere. Open source changes that. Anyone can study it, run it, fix it, and build on it. What is often missing is not the software. It is people at home who know it well and can teach it.

## The model: train one, they train many
1. We run a one-day Saturday session for working technology practitioners.
2. Each person goes back to work and teaches the same material to their co-workers.
3. Some of those co-workers become trainers too.

We pick practitioners, not professors, on purpose. They already use this technology every day, they know what really breaks, and they can teach it on Monday.

## The courses
Each course is one Saturday. Every course folder has an outline, a timed agenda, labs, and a facilitator guide.

| # | Course | You will be able to |
|---|--------|---------------------|
| 1 | [Getting started with open source](courses/01-open-source-basics/) | Choose a license, join a community, make a first contribution, explain governance |
| 2 | [Beginning AI with open-source tools](courses/02-open-source-ai/) | Run a small open model on a cheap laptop, with no cloud account, and build a simple "ask your documents" tool |
| 3 | [How to train others](courses/03-how-to-train/) | Teach adults, run a hands-on lab, and plan your own cascade |

For self-paced study, the [curriculum](curriculum/) has 12 modules, from FOSS basics to InnerSource, community building, AI workflows, and funding.

The [trainer kit](trainer-kit/) has a cascade plan, a session checklist, and feedback forms.

## Built for low bandwidth
- Everything is plain text (Markdown). It reads fine on a phone and prints well.
- Labs list every download up front so you can fetch them once, on a good connection, and copy them by USB stick.
- No lab needs a paid account.
