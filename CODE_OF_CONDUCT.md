# Code of Conduct

We want every session and every discussion to be welcoming to people of every country, language, gender, religion, age, ability, and level of experience.

## We expect
- Respect, patience, and kindness, especially toward beginners.
- Good-faith questions and answers. There are no stupid questions here.
- Credit for other people's work.

## We do not accept
- Harassment, insults, or threats, in person or online.
- Unwanted sexual attention.
- Mocking someone's accent, English, or experience level.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.

## At sessions
The lead facilitator is the contact person for the day. Say their name at the start.

## Reporting
Email the maintainers at: tim@serewicz.com (to be set). Reports are kept private. We may warn, remove, or ban people who break this code.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
