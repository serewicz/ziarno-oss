# Outline: How to train others

## By the end of the day, each person can
1. Plan a short session using how adults learn best.
2. Write a clear, numbered lab that someone else can follow.
3. Run a hands-on lab and handle problems in the room.
4. Give and take useful feedback.
5. Plan their own cascade: who, when, and how they will know it worked.

## Modules
1. **How adults learn.** Adults learn best when it is useful now, builds on what they know, and lets them try it. Talk less, practice more.
2. **Planning a session.** Start with "By the end, you can..." Then pick activities that get people there.
3. **Writing labs.** One action per step. Name the exact button. Test it on someone who has never seen it.
4. **Running the room.** Timing, helping people who are stuck without taking their keyboard, pairing fast and slow learners.
5. **Feedback.** Ask, listen, and change the material.
6. **Cascading.** Your plan to train your co-workers within the next month.
