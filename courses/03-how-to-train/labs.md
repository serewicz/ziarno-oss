# Labs: How to train others

## Lab 1: Plan a 20-minute session (45 min)
1. Pick one topic from Course 1 or 2 that you will teach at work.
2. Finish this sentence: "By the end, you will be able to ________." Use an action word (run, explain, choose, fix).
3. Split 20 minutes: about 5 minutes explaining, about 12 minutes doing, about 3 minutes checking what they learned.
4. Write what you will need: room, laptops, files, internet.
5. Show your plan to a partner. Partner asks: "How will you know they learned it?"

## Lab 2: Write a lab, then swap and test (60 min)
1. Write a lab of 5 to 10 numbered steps for your topic.
2. Each step has one action. Name the exact button, menu, or command.
3. Swap with a partner. Your partner follows your steps exactly. No talking.
4. Watch. Every time they hesitate, mark that step.
5. Fix the marked steps. Swap back and do theirs.

## Lab 3: Teach your session (75 min)
Groups of 4. Each person teaches their 20-minute session, cut to 12 minutes.
1. Teacher teaches. Others act as learners.
2. One person keeps time and gives a 2-minute warning.
3. After each session, use the peer feedback form in `trainer-kit/feedback-forms.md`: one thing that worked, one thing to change.
4. Teacher only listens and says "thank you." No defending.
