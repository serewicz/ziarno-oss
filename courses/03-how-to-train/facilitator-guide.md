# Facilitator guide: How to train others

- Model what you teach: talk less than half the day.
- Lab 2 is the most important lab in the program. Do not cut it short.
- Some people are nervous to teach. Let them go second or third, not first.
- Keep feedback specific and kind: "When you showed the screen slowly, I could follow" beats "Good job."
- End the day with everyone writing a date for their first cascade session in `trainer-kit/cascade-plan.md`.
