# Mission

**Train technology leaders in emerging countries so they can train others, and move their countries from big-tech dependence to open source.**

## What we believe
- A country should be able to own, run, and repair its own technology.
- The fastest way to spread a skill is to teach someone who will teach it next.
- Working practitioners make the best trainers, because they know what really happens at work.
- Training material should be free to use, change, translate, and share.

## What we do
- Run one-day Saturday train-the-trainer sessions.
- Publish all course material openly.
- Help each trainer plan and run training for their co-workers.

## What success looks like
Trainers in each country run sessions without us, adapt the material to local needs, and send their improvements back here.
